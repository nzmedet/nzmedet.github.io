Privacy policy.

Effective 13 September 2026

CELL is an interactive human cell atlas provided by MEDET TLEUGABYLULY LIMITED. This policy covers the CELL app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android, and these CELL website pages.

Using the app

CELL does not require an account. The atlas, 3D models, explanatory text, and animations are included in the app and run on your device. You can explore them offline.

We do not collect your cell selections, camera movements, or animation interactions. CELL contains no advertising and does not enable app analytics or a third-party tracking service. It does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library.

Information on your device

The app and its rendering engine may keep local preferences, caches, and diagnostic logs needed to run the app. CELL does not upload those local files to us. Your device’s app-management settings control the app’s local storage.

Scientific references

When you choose a scientific source in the app, CELL opens that website in your browser. The external site receives the usual browser request information and handles it under its own privacy policy. CELL does not send your exploration history with the link.

Contacting support

If you email nzmedet@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that is not needed for your request.

We do not sell support information or use it for advertising. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.

Apple and Google services

Store downloads, payment processing, and any diagnostics you choose to share with your platform provider are handled under that provider’s policies and your device settings. See Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies. Fonts and images are served with the site. Following an App Store or other external link takes you to that provider’s service.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email nzmedet@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when the policy changes.
