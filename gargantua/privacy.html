Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026

This policy covers the Gargantua Mac app provided by MEDET TLEUGABYLULY LIMITED.

The app

Gargantua runs its visualization on your Mac. It does not require an account and does not send your screenshots or camera interactions to us. It contains no advertising or third-party tracking service.

Files on your Mac

When you choose Screenshot, Gargantua saves a PNG locally in its application storage and reveals it in Finder. Local settings and diagnostic logs may also be stored on your Mac. You control whether to copy, share, or delete your saved images.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use those messages to respond to your request and investigate the issue. We do not sell support information or use it for advertising. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

Apple services

App Store downloads and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are handled under Apple's privacy policies and your system settings.

This website

The support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website. These pages do not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

Contact and changes

Contact nzmedet@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. We will update this page and its effective date if the policy changes.
