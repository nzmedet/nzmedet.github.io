GARGANTUA

A cinematic black hole viewer for your Mac.

Explore glowing gas and gravitational lensing, frame a view, and save it as a wallpaper. Gargantua is an artistic visualization inspired by black hole physics.

Getting started

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later.

  • Left-click and drag to orbit, with unlimited vertical rotation.
  • Right-click and drag to pan.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Reset View recenters the camera.
  • Use the toolbar to select 30 or 60 FPS and change visual quality.
  • Press H to hide the interface, F for fullscreen, or R to reset.

Save a wallpaper

Click Screenshot to save a clean PNG without the interface. Finder reveals the saved image. You can copy it elsewhere or select it in macOS Wallpaper settings.

Performance

Rendering a black hole uses your GPU. The default 30 FPS setting reduces the workload. Choose Performance quality for a lighter experience. Rendering pauses when the app is in the background.
