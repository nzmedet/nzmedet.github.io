Website privacy

Effective 17 September 2026

This policy covers the pages on tsx.nz operated by MEDET TLEUGABYLULY LIMITED. Each app and separately hosted service has its own privacy policy.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page views, referral sources, approximate location, browser and device types, engagement, scrolling, and outbound links such as App Store buttons. Google uses cookies and processes technical information about your visit to provide these reports. A store-link click does not tell us whether you downloaded or purchased an app.

With no saved choice, Analytics stays off until you accept if your country is in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or the associated territories covered by our opt-in settings. It also stays off if we cannot determine your country. For other recognised countries, Analytics starts automatically after the country check, with a notice and a Turn off option. Ignoring that notice leaves Analytics on; ignoring an opt-in prompt leaves it off. A saved choice is respected in every region. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep Analytics off. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in our website tag. We do not send names, email addresses, or account details to Analytics intentionally, and there is no website login.

Learn how Google processes information from sites that use its services. Detailed user and event history is set to 14-month retention, without extending user-data retention when you return. Aggregate reports may be kept longer.

Your choices

You can accept, decline, or turn off analytics, and change your choice using Analytics preferences at the bottom of any page. Declining or choosing Turn off keeps Analytics off while your saved choice remains valid. Withdrawing consent stops future Analytics collection and removes its cookies from this website; it does not delete data already received by Google.

Your choice is stored in your browser for up to six months. Analytics cookies are also set to expire after six months. Clearing browser storage may cause us to ask again. If your browser blocks storage, we can honour your choice only for the current page.

Google processes Analytics data on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, Google’s privacy policy, and Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Country detection

When you have no saved analytics choice and your browser has not signalled an opt-out, we request Cloudflare’s public country lookup to choose the appropriate notice. Cloudflare receives your IP address and the technical information needed to answer. We use only the country code, do not save the returned IP address, and send no cookies or page referrer with this request. No precise device location is requested. A failed or slow lookup keeps Analytics off until you accept. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Hosting and other requests

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect it. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the homepage loads its animation library from jsDelivr. These providers receive the technical information needed to deliver those files regardless of your analytics choice. External links open services that follow their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email nzmedet@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when our website data practices change.
